A directory of community plugins for
[Hugo Studio](https://github.com/hugo-tools/hugo-studio).

The same content tree this site is built from is also published as a
JSON feed at [`/plugins/index.json`](/plugins/index.json) — that's
what the editor's "Browse" tab consumes when you click "Install".
